What are snippets?

A snippet is a user-authored labeled example. Positive examples show what good looks like; negative examples show off-topic content the relevance gate should drop. Snippets are used two ways from the same data: as few-shot examples injected into the LLM prompts (so the model sees concrete examples of your topic), and as eval gold for the eval harness when holdout is enabled.
