How this works

The assistant LLM (see assistant LLM settings) reviews your current prompts against your most recent snippets and proposes targeted edits — add, remove, replace. Each edit is dry-run against your last 30 days of ingested items: if applying it would drop the relevance pass rate by more than 10pp, the checkbox is disabled to prevent accidental regressions. Applied edits archive the previous prompts to products/{{ product.id }}/prompts.yaml.history/v<N>.yaml and bump the version so old classifications remain reproducible.
