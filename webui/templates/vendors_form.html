What each field means

Canonical: the vendor's primary name as you want it stored in the warehouse. e.g. "Intel", "Dolby", "NVIDIA".

Aliases: comma-separated alternate spellings users actually type. e.g. for Intel: "intel corp, intel corporation". For Dolby: "dolby labs, dolby laboratories". The regex pre-pass matches any alias and normalizes to the canonical name.

Types: comma-separated entity types this vendor is associated with. Used as a hint to the classifier when a vendor match shows up. Common types: gpu, cpu, chipset, motherboard, laptop, desktop, audio_device, audio_technology, network_adapter, bluetooth_adapter, driver, firmware, app, display, webcam.

Products: comma-separated product names this vendor makes that you care about. e.g. for Intel: "AX211, AX210, Arc, Iris Xe, Core". These get matched verbatim during regex extraction.

Active: untick to keep an entry around without it being used (faster than deleting if you might re-enable later).
